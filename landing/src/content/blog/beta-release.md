Our app is in public beta! You can [install it](/download) on your phone,
laptop, or other devices. It takes screenshots at random, runs them through a
classifier, blurs them, and then sends them to your partners, who can follow up
if needed. Our app is ready for beta testing on all the main platforms:
Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android. We need your feedback!

> Note: Due to iOS limitations, we can only capture screenshots inside Safari
> on iPhone.

## Who are we?

We started this project in February 2026. We are a nonprofit (working towards
501(c)(3) status), and our goal is to provide a free app and hopefully other
resources for people to break free from pornography. We hate that it is ruining
so many people's lives, and we want to reduce the obstacles and excuses that
keep people from dealing with it.

No app is going to solve all your problems, but an accountability app with
someone you trust can be a really good first step. We want it to be as easy as
possible to take that first step.

## How can you help?

We are still early in the project, and there are a few things you can do to
help.

1. **[Install the app](https://app.virtueinitiative.org/signup)**. Even if you
   do not struggle with pornography, installing the app and giving us feedback
   is one of the most helpful things right now. We need to know what works well
   and what does not so that we can improve it.

2. **[Donate](https://virtueinitiative.org/donate)**. We are not a 501(c)(3)
   yet, so donations are not tax deductible. We are working toward 501(c)(3)
   status, but getting it is expensive (lawyers, filing fees, etc.). If you
   are able and want to support our mission, we could use some money to help
   get this off the ground.

   > Note: We are organized as a nonprofit in New York and have a board, so we
   > are required to operate as a nonprofit and must use any funds we receive
   > to further our mission, but you cannot write off donations as tax
   > deductible yet (which only matters if you do not take the standard
   > deduction).

3. **Tell others about the app**. The core app is already built, but the biggest
   challenge will likely be helping people learn that there is a free
   option. We do not want cost to be the reason people do not take the first
   step, so tell them about it!

4. **Get your church or organization involved**. We want to partner with
   churches and other organizations. If your organization is interested in
   partnering with us, feel free to reach out at
   [partner@virtueinitiative.org](mailto:partner@virtueinitiative.org).

5. **Pray for us**. Little will change without prayer. Pray that people would
   be freed from pornography and that we would be able to provide the tools to help
   them.

Lastly, if you have any questions, thoughts, or comments, reach out to us at
[help@virtueinitiative.org](mailto:help@virtueinitiative.org). We promise we
will read your email.
