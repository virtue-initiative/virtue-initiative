Our app is in public beta! You can install it on your phone, laptop, or other devices. It takes screenshots at
random, runs them through a classifier, blurs them, and then sends them to your
partners, who can follow up if needed. Our app is ready for beta testing on all
the main platforms: Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android. We need your
feedback!

> Note: Due to iOS limitations, we can only capture screenshots inside Safari
> on iPhone.

## Who are we?

We started this project in February 2026. We are a nonprofit (still awaiting
501(c)(3) status), and our goal is to provide a free app and hopefully other
resources for people to break free from pornography. We hate that it is ruining
so many people's lives, and we want to reduce the obstacles and excuses that
keep people from dealing with it until it is too late.

No app is going to solve all your problems, but an accountability app with
someone you trust can be a really good first step. We want it to be as easy as
possible to take that first step.

## How can you help?

We are still early in the project, and there are a few practical things you can
do to help.

1. **[Install the app](https://app.virtueinitiative.org/signup)**. Even if you
   don't struggle with this, installing the app and giving us feedback is one
   of the most helpful things right now. We need to know what works well and
   what doesn't so that we can improve it.

2. **[Donate](https://virtueinitiative.org/donate)**. We are not a 501(c)(3)
   yet, so donations are not tax deductible. We are working toward 501(c)(3)
   status, but getting it is expensive (lawyers, filing fees, etc.). If you
   want and are able to support our mission, we could use some money to help
   get this off the ground.

   > Note: We are organized as a nonprofit in New York and have a board, so we
   > are required to operate as a nonprofit and must use any funds we receive
   > to further our mission. You just cannot write off donations as
   > tax deductible yet (which only matters if you do not take the standard
   > deduction).

3. **Tell others about the app**. The core app is already mostly built. The biggest
   challenge is likely going to be helping people learn that there is a free
   option. We do not want cost to be the reason people do not take the first
   step, so tell them about it! Spread the word!

Lastly, if you have any questions, thoughts, or comments, feel free to reach out
to us at [help@virtueinitiative.org](mailto:help@virtueinitiative.org). We
promise we will read your email.
