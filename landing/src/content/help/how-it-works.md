# How it works

Our main accountability tool is an app that takes screenshots at random
intervals. When you install [Virtue](/download) on your phone or computer it
starts capturing screenshots about every 5 minutes on average. It shrinks and
blurs the screenshots and blacks out any detected text to increase privacy (and
protect your partner). It also performs a check on-device to detect potential
concern in images. It keeps these screenshots on your device and then uploads
them in a secure encrypted batch about once an hour. See [what Virtue
monitors](/help/what-virtue-monitors) for the details on each device.

> Note: On iOS the screenshots can only be captured inside the Safari browser
> due to Apple's limitations. We have specific instructions on [how to block
> downloading other browsers on
> iOS](/help/removing-access/block-app-installs-ios).

Any partners you have added will periodically receive an email (daily or weekly)
reminding them to review your screenshots. If they see anything questionable,
they can contact you to discuss it. The idea of our app is to help start
conversations and provide motivation to avoid pornography.
