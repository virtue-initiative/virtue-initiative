---
title: 'Deleting your account'
description: 'Permanently delete your account, devices and activity.'
topic: account
order: 6
---

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Delete account**.
3. Type your account email to confirm, then click **Delete account**.

Virtue signs you out and deletes your account, your devices, and your activity. The encrypted
screenshot files themselves are removed within 30 days.

Uninstall Virtue from your devices afterwards. See [uninstalling Virtue](/help/uninstalling-virtue).
