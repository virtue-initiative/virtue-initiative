# Getting started

To get set up, all you have to do is:

1. [Create an account](/signup). Register for a free account on
   virtueinitiative.org.
2. [Install one of the monitoring apps](/download). We support Windows, Mac,
   iOS, Android, and Linux.
3. [Invite a partner to your account](/help/web/inviting-a-partner). Find
   someone you trust and invite them to hold you accountable. The goal of
   accountability is not to bring shame, but to encourage each other to live with
   integrity.
