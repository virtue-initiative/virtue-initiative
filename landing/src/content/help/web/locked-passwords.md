# Locked passwords

A locked password stores a secret that you might need to access, but generally
should not, like a Screen Time passcode. You can find it on the **Passwords**
page.

## Create one

Click **Add password**, name it, and enter the secret value. The value is
encrypted in your browser before it is sent. Nobody but you can decrypt it,
not even Virtue's servers.

## Reveal it

Click **Reveal** to see the value again. The first time you reveal a password,
it notifies your partners and the entry is permanently marked **Accessed**.
They are told which password was accessed, but never the value itself.

Revealing it again after that does not trigger another alert.

## Delete it

Click **Delete** to remove a password. It moves to **Recently deleted** for 7
days, where you can restore it or delete it permanently. After 7 days it is
deleted permanently on its own.
