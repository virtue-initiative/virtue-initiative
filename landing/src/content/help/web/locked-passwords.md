# Locked passwords

A locked password stores a secret you don't want easy access to yourself,
like a Screen Time passcode. Find it on the **Passwords** page.

## Create one

Click **Add password**, name it, and enter the secret value. The value is
encrypted in your browser before it's sent. Nobody but you can decrypt it,
not even Virtue's servers.

## Reveal it

Click **Reveal** to see the value again. The first time you reveal a
password, it's a red flag: the entry is permanently marked **Accessed**, and
every partner watching you is emailed immediately. They're told which
password was accessed, never the value itself.

Revealing it again after that doesn't trigger another alert. The flag can't
be cleared.

## Delete it

Click **Delete** to remove a password. It moves to **Recently deleted** for 7
days, where you can restore it or delete it permanently. After 7 days it's
deleted permanently on its own.

## Why partners get no access

Locked passwords are meant to work against you, not for your partners to
manage. Partners can't see, list, or reveal them. Their only role is
receiving the alert if you ever reveal one yourself.
