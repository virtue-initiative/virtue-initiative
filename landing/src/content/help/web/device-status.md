# Device status

Every device on the Devices page or listed on a partner's card carries a
status tag to indicate if the device is still sending information, and if
it still set up at all.

## Online

The device uploaded a batch of activity within the last two hours.

This is the normal state for a device that's in use. A device that's switched
off, asleep, or offline for the night will leave this state without anything
being wrong.

## Offline

The device is still set up, but its last upload was more than two hours ago.

This is common and usually harmless: the computer or phone is off, sleeping,
or has no internet connection. It will flip back to **Online** on its own
once the device uploads again. No action needed on your side.

Offline stops being routine when it lasts. A device that's offline for days
while the person is clearly using it is worth asking about: the app may have
been stopped, blocked from the network, or removed from startup.

The **(last seen)** time next to each device tells you how long it has been
offline.

## Deactivated

Someone logged the device out. It is no longer being monitored, and it will not
come back on its own.

This is the one status that always means a deliberate action was taken. Logging
out is not something that happens by accident or through a bad connection, so
a device that's moved from Online to Deactivated has been signed out by whoever
has access to it.

Both you and your partners are emailed when a device logs out, so this status
shouldn't be the first you hear of it. If you didn't expect it, that's worth a
conversation.

To start monitoring on a computer or phone that has been deactivated, log in on it.
That registers it as a **new** device on the website, listed separately.
Delete the deactivated device record from the Devices page when you no longer need it.

The deactivated device's existing logs stay where they are until you delete it;
deactivating on its own removes nothing. Deleting the device does remove its
stored batches, so leave it in place if you still want its history.
