# Concern scores

Every screenshot in your log feed is automatically scored for concern by an
on-device classifier. This is not a person's judgment. It's an automated
estimate meant to help you find the entries most worth a closer look.

## Levels

Each score is shown as a percentage alongside one of four levels:

- **Low** — below 40%
- **Medium** — 40% to 70%
- **High** — 70% to 90%
- **Alert** — 90% and above

## Alert-level emails

When a screenshot scores in the Alert range, an email notifying accountability
partners is sent immediately, rather than waiting for the next regular
summary. This is meant to surface the small number of events that are most
likely to need prompt attention.
