# Tips

Here are some tips on how to beat pornography.

1. **Do not do this alone**. This is not a fight you win by yourself. Find
   someone you trust and start talking to them about it. You are not the only
   one who has struggled before.
2. **Do not give up**. It is tempting to give up, and you might feel like you
   will never get over it, but it is possible! Do not give in to hopelessness.
   Despair is as dangerous as the temptation itself.
3. [**Remove access**](/help/removing-access). Get rid of as many avenues to
   pornography as you can. This might mean disabling the web browser on your
   phone, setting up filters, or getting rid of your phone altogether. This has
   limits, but it does help.
4. **Avoid temptation**. Consider keeping track of or thinking about places or
   times where you are normally tempted and make a plan for avoiding the
   temptation at those times and places.
5. **Monitor access**. If you cannot remove access, monitor it. That is where
   screenshot software like [Virtue](/help/how-it-works) comes in. Since it is
   the 21st century, at some point you will probably need access to the
   internet. Remember, this is only one brick in your wall against pornography,
   but it is still important.
6. **Be patient with yourself**. This is not a struggle that goes away
   overnight. It will likely take longer than you hope. You will not be able to
   fight it perfectly. Remember to keep going.

Each of these things is one line of defense. Combined, they give you a much
better chance of resisting than any one of them alone.
