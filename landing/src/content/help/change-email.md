---
title: 'Changing your email'
description: 'Move your account to a new email address and confirm it.'
topic: account
order: 2
---

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
2. Enter the new address in the **Email** field and click **Verify**.
3. Open the email Virtue sends to the new address and click the link in it.

Your account keeps using the old address until you click that link. Once you do, Virtue shows
**Email updated**, and you log in with the new address from then on.

If the new address has bounced an email from Virtue before, **Settings** says so and sends
nothing. Check the address for typos, or use a different one.
