---

1. Open the [sign-up page](/signup), enter your email, and click **Send verification email**.
2. Open the email from Virtue and click the link in it. The link takes you back to Virtue to
   finish signing up.
3. Enter your name if you want partners to see it, then choose a password of at least 12
   characters and type it again.
4. Tick the box to agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, then click **Create account**.

You are signed in as soon as the account is created.

## Choose a password you will remember

Signing up creates an encryption key for your account, and your password protects it. If you
forget your password and reset it, you can no longer read the activity uploaded before the
reset. See [changing or resetting your password](/help/reset-password).

## Next steps

1. [Install Virtue](/download) on each computer and phone you want to monitor.
2. [Invite a partner](/help/web/inviting-a-partner) to see your activity.
