# What Virtue monitors

Virtue takes screenshots at random times, about every 5 minutes on average. It
blacks out text, shrinks and blurs each screenshot, and encrypts it so only you
and your partners can see it. What gets captured depends on the device.

- [Windows](/help/what-virtue-monitors/windows): every monitor.
- [Mac](/help/what-virtue-monitors/mac): the main display.
- [Linux](/help/what-virtue-monitors/linux): all screens.
- [Android](/help/what-virtue-monitors/android): the whole screen.
- [iPhone and iPad](/help/what-virtue-monitors/ios): only web pages in Safari.
