# What Virtue monitors on Android

This page lists everything the Virtue app on Android captures and sends.

Virtue uses the Accessibility permission only to take screenshots. It does not read what you type or tap.

## Screenshots

- Virtue takes a screenshot of your whole screen at random times, about every 5 minutes on average.
- No screenshot is taken while the screen is off.
- If the screen has not changed since the last screenshot, Virtue sends a note saying so instead of a new screenshot.

## Privacy

- Before a screenshot leaves this device, Virtue blacks out any text it finds, then shrinks and blurs the image.
- Virtue rates each screenshot for explicit content on this device. See [concern scores](/help/web/concern-scores).
- Screenshots are encrypted so only you and your partners can see them. See [security and encryption](/help/developer/security).

## Other reports

- Virtue reports when monitoring is paused or stopped, and when a screenshot is missed or fails.
- Virtue reports when this device restarts.
- Virtue sends a daily check-in to show it is running.

## What Virtue does not record

Other than screenshots, Virtue does not record keystrokes, website addresses, app names, window titles, audio, or location.
