# What Virtue monitors on iPhone and iPad

This page lists everything the Virtue app on iPhone and iPad captures and sends.

Apple only lets Virtue capture Safari, so Virtue works as a Safari extension. See
[block app installs](/help/removing-access/block-app-installs-ios) to keep other
browsers off your device.

## Screenshots

- While you use Safari, Virtue takes a screenshot of the web page on screen at random times, about every 5 minutes on average.
- Other apps, the Home Screen, and Safari tabs you are not viewing are not captured.
- Private Browsing tabs are captured when the extension is allowed in Private Browsing.
- If the page has not changed since the last screenshot, Virtue sends a note saying so instead of a new screenshot.

## Privacy

- Before a screenshot leaves your device, Virtue blacks out any text it finds, then shrinks and blurs the image.
- Virtue rates each screenshot for explicit content on your device. See [concern scores](/help/web/concern-scores).
- Screenshots are encrypted so only you and your partners can see them. See [security and encryption](/help/developer/security).
- The address and title of the current page appear in Status Details on your device. They are never uploaded.

## Other reports

- Virtue reports when monitoring is paused and when a screenshot fails.
- While Safari is in use, Virtue sends a daily check-in to show it is running.

## What Virtue does not record

Other than screenshots, Virtue does not record keystrokes, audio, or location.
