# What Virtue monitors on Mac

This page lists everything the Virtue app on Mac captures and sends.

Other displays are not captured yet. Virtue only captures the display macOS treats as the main one.

## Screenshots

- Virtue takes a screenshot of your main display at random times, about every 5 minutes on average.
- No screenshot is taken while the screen is locked or the screensaver is on.
- If the screen has not changed since the last screenshot, Virtue sends a note saying so instead of a new screenshot.

## Privacy

- Before a screenshot leaves this Mac, Virtue blacks out any text it finds, then shrinks and blurs the image.
- Virtue rates each screenshot for explicit content on this Mac. See [concern scores](/help/web/concern-scores).
- Screenshots are encrypted so only you and your partners can see them. See [security and encryption](/help/developer/security).

## Other reports

- Virtue reports when monitoring is paused or stopped, and when a screenshot is missed or fails.
- Virtue reports when you log in to or out of your Mac.
- Virtue sends a daily check-in to show it is running.

## What Virtue does not record

Other than screenshots, Virtue does not record keystrokes, website addresses, app names, window titles, audio, or location.
