Uninstalling Virtue itself sends nothing to your partners. The device just stops checking in, so
it shows as **Offline** and their summary emails name it. On a Mac you stop monitoring first,
which sends your partners a **Monitoring Stopped by User** email straight away. If you are removing
Virtue for a good reason, such as replacing the device, tell your partners first.

## Remove the app

- **Windows**: follow the uninstall steps on the [download page](/download#windows).
- **Mac**: open Virtue and click **Stop Monitoring & Quit**, which tells your partners. Then drag
  Virtue from your Applications folder to the Trash.
- **Linux**: run `sudo apt remove virtue`.
- **Android**: touch and hold the Virtue icon, then tap **Uninstall**.
- **iPhone and iPad**: touch and hold the Virtue icon, then tap **Remove App**.

## Remove the device from your account

The device stays on your **Devices** page after the app is gone. Click **Edit** on its card,
then **Delete device permanently**, if you no longer need its activity. Your partners are told
when a device is deleted.
