---
title: 'Accepting a partner invite'
description: 'Accept an invite so you can see someone’s activity, with or without an account.'
topic: partners
order: 2
---

When someone invites you to be their partner, Virtue sends you an email with a link.

1. Click the link in the invite email.
2. Log in. If you do not have an account yet, sign up with the same email address the invite was
   sent to. Virtue brings you back to the invite afterwards.
3. Virtue accepts the invite and shows who it was from.
4. If this is your first partner, choose how often you want summary emails, **Daily** or
   **Weekly**, then click **Done**.

The person who invited you now appears on your **Partners** page under **You monitor**. Click
**View logs** on their card, or pick their name under **Logs** in the sidebar, to see their
activity. You can see what they upload from now on, not anything from before you accepted.

## If the invite does not work

If the page shows **Invite error**, the link may have expired or already been used. Ask the
person to remove the pending invite and send a new one.

## Changing your summary emails

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Settings**. **Email frequency** sets how often you hear
about the people you monitor: **None**, **Alerts only**, **Daily** or **Weekly**.
