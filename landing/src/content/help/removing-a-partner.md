---
title: 'Removing a partner'
description: 'End a partnership or cancel a pending invite from the Partners page.'
topic: partners
order: 3
---

1. Open the **Partners** page from the sidebar.
2. Find the person's card. People who can see your activity are under **Monitor you**. People
   whose activity you can see are under **You monitor**.
3. Click **Remove**, then confirm.

The partnership ends straight away, and the other person is told that it ended.

To cancel an invite that has not been accepted yet, click **Remove** on the card marked
**Pending**.
