If you know your password, change it in **Settings**. Only reset it if you have forgotten it,
because a reset makes your older activity unreadable.

## Change your password

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Password**, enter your current password, then your new password twice. It needs at
   least 12 characters.
3. Click **Change password**.

Your logs stay readable, your devices stay signed in, and any other browsers you were signed in
on are signed out.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the log in page, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email from Virtue and click the link.
4. Choose a new password, type it again, and click **Reset password**.

You are signed in once the reset is done.

A reset creates a new encryption key for your account, because the old one was protected by the
password you lost. You can no longer read the activity your devices uploaded before the reset.
Sign out of Virtue on each of your devices and sign back in, so they use the new key.
