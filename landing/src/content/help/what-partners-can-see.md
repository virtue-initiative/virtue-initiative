A partner sees everything your devices upload after they accept your invite. Nothing from before
that is shared.

## In the web app

- **Logs**: your screenshots and every other entry in your log feed, such as sign-ins and alerts.
  See [log types](/help/web/log-types).
- **Reports**: a summary of a day or week, with the entries most worth a look.
- **Devices**: each of your devices on your card on their **Partners** page, with its
  [status](/help/web/device-status) and when it was last seen.

Screenshots are shrunk, blurred, and have their text blacked out before they leave your device,
so partners see a rough picture of the screen, not what you were reading. See
[what Virtue monitors](/help/what-virtue-monitors) for what each device captures.

## By email

Each partner chooses in **Settings** how often they hear from Virtue:

- **Daily** or **Weekly** summaries give the number of screenshots and alerts for each person
  they monitor, and name any device that sent nothing.
- Some events are emailed straight away, whatever the summary setting, unless the partner chose
  **None**: stopping monitoring in the app, signing a device out, and revealing a
  [locked password](/help/web/locked-passwords).

## What partners cannot see

- Anything uploaded before they accepted your invite.
- The value of a locked password. They are told only that you revealed it, and which one.
- Your password, or anything in your account settings.
