## Mac installation

1. If you do not have one, [create an account](/signup).
2. Download the [Mac `.dmg` file]({MAC_DOWNLOAD}).
3. Open the `.dmg` and drag the Virtue icon to Applications folder.
4. Open the app and login
5. After login, you should be prompted to allow access for taking screenshots. If that does not work, you can manually open System Preferences then search for "recording". Look for Virtue in the list of applications requesting permissions and enable the switch next to it.
6. After enabling permissions, click the "restart monitoring service" button within the Virtue app.
7. Done! The app will periodically collect screenshots (about once every 5 minutes) and upload them once an hour. You and your partners will be able to view them from the logs page on the website.
