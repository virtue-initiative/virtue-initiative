## iOS installation

The iOS app is currently available via TestFlight beta only.

1. Install [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) from the App Store.
2. Open the [Virtue TestFlight invitation]({IOS_DOWNLOAD}) on your device and tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
3. Open Virtue and sign in.

Contact us at [help@virtueinitiative.org](mailto:help@virtueinitiative.org) if you run into trouble.
